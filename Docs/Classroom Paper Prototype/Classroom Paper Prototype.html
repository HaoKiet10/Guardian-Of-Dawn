Classroom Paper Prototype: "Collect the Papers"

2 levels · 1 player · turn-based board version. Print pages 3–5, cut out the tokens on page 5, and play on the maps.

Goal

  • Collect every paper in the classroom, then walk out the EXIT door.

Map pieces (fixed)

  • Desk (brown rectangle): obstacle. Nobody walks through it.
  • Teacher Desk (dark brown): obstacle.
  • Chair (blue square): where students sit. You can walk over it.
  • Paper (yellow note, P1…): step on it to collect it.
  • Power-up (green circle): step on it to pick it up.
  • START / EXIT: doors. EXIT stays locked until all papers are collected.

Turn order

  1. Student turn: move up to 3 squares (up/down/left/right, no diagonals). You may use 1 held power-up.
  2. Witch turn: every witch moves 2 squares along its dashed route, following the arrow. At the end of a back-and-forth route it turns around; a loop route keeps going round.
  3. Mark one box on the turn tracker.

Witches: "Sit down!"

  • If a witch is on or next to (orthogonally) the student at the end of any move, the student is caught.
  • Caught: move the student to the nearest free Chair and sit out 2 turns (Level 2: 3 turns). Witches keep moving.
  • A seated student can't be caught again.
  • Hide: you can choose to sit on a chair on purpose: witches ignore you, but you skip your next turn.

Power-ups (hold max 1)

  • Speed (Sneakers): move 5 squares for your next 3 turns.
  • Pass (Hall Pass): witches can't catch you for 3 turns.
  • Bell (Freeze Bell, Level 2): all witches skip their next 2 turns.
  • Magnet (Level 2): instantly collect every paper within 2 squares.

Level 2 extras

  • Head Witch sits at the Teacher Desk and never moves. Ending a move in her red watch zone = caught.
  • Bell rings at turn 30: if you haven't left, you lose.

Level 1: First Period

Tutorial. Open aisles, two predictable witches. Start bottom-left, exit top-right.

Pieces

  • 12 Desks, 1 Teacher Desk
  • 6 Papers: P1–P6
  • 4 Chairs
  • W1: back-and-forth along row 5
  • W2: back-and-forth down column H
  • Power-ups: Speed (D9), Pass (N7)

Design intent

  • P1 and P6 sit far from both witches: safe first pickups.
  • P3 (H6) sits on W2's lane: teaches timing.
  • W1 cuts the room in half at row 5: you must cross it at least once.
  • Pass (N7) is next to W1's turn-around: reward for a risk.
  • Chairs at C4 and J4 sit right by W1's row, so a catch there costs little distance.

Turn tracker

Papers

☐ P1 ☐ P2 ☐ P3 ☐ P4 ☐ P5 ☐ P6 · ★ in 20 turns or fewer

Level 2: Exam Day

Tighter rows, dead ends, 3 patrolling witches + the Head Witch. 30-turn limit.

Pieces

  • 18 Desks, 1 Teacher Desk
  • 10 Papers: P1–P10
  • 3 Chairs
  • W1: back-and-forth along row 2 (front)
  • W2: back-and-forth along row 6
  • W3: loop around the back (rows 8–10)
  • Head Witch: fixed, watch zone E2–J2
  • Power-ups: Speed (G4), Pass (A7), Bell (N7), Magnet (J8)

Design intent

  • Rows 5 and 9 leave only 4 gaps each: you have to plan which gap to use.
  • P2 (F3) is just below the watch zone: reach it from the side.
  • Magnet (J8) grabs P8 and P9 at once, but it sits on W3's loop.
  • Bell (N7) freezes everyone: save it for the front row and the exit run.
  • Only 3 chairs: getting caught sends you further away.
